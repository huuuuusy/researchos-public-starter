# How I do one kind of task

## Agent Summary

One or two sentences: which kind of task this card covers and the two or three points that matter most; read it before drafting that kind of task, and do not treat the assistant's additions as rules.

## What I do (with source)

- The rule in one line, e.g. how a paragraph opens, what a good figure caption does.
  - Why: the reason you gave, or the correction it came from.
  - How to apply: where it applies, and where it does not.
  - Source: the date, and where the correction or example is.

## What I cut or avoid (with source)

- Something you remove from drafts, or a word you avoid.
  - Why: the reason you gave, or the correction it came from.
  - How to apply: where it applies, and where it does not.
  - Source: the date, and where the correction or example is.

## Examples from my own text

- A short passage of your own that you consider good, where it is, and one line on why it works. Only your own text, never someone else's.

## Assistant's additions (not confirmed by me)

- Patterns an assistant thinks it sees in your texts; not relied on until you confirm them and move them up with a source.

## To verify

- Points you are unsure still hold, or that hold only in some settings.

## Evidence boundary

- This card is based on: which of your texts, comments and advice, and where they are.
