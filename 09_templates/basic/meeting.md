# YYYY-MM-DD Meeting name

## Agent Summary

## Record (fact)

- When, format, length, participants, where the material is.

## What was said (fact, by speaker)

- One line per point, saying who said it.

## My view

- Your own reading and plans, written apart from what others said. To keep it as a durable judgment, propose it with `ros propose judgment`; it goes into a card only after you confirm.

## Assistant's additions (not confirmed by me)

- An assistant's guesses go here and are not relied on until confirmed.

- Decisions made at the meeting. If the reasons and options are worth keeping, write a decision card and link it.
