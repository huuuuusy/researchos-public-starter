# Short description of the message

## Agent Summary

One or two sentences: who it is for, why, and the one thing it asks; read this card when drafting or checking the message, not as a record of what was actually sent.

## Purpose and the one ask

- Why you are writing, and the single thing you want the recipient to do or answer.

## Facts it relies on (fact, with source)

- Every date, number and earlier agreement the message mentions, each with where it comes from (a card, an email, a meeting).

## Draft

The text, short. Keep the tone you would use with this person.

## Assistant's additions (not confirmed by me)

- Wording or points an assistant suggested and you have not yet accepted.

## To verify

- Facts to check before sending.

## Evidence boundary

- This card records: the draft and the facts it relies on.
- Not checked:
- The sent text stays in your mail; note the date it was sent here.
