# What does not go out, and who approves

## Agent Summary

One or two sentences that stand on their own: the main kinds of material never given to an assistant, the topics not said outside, and who approves before something goes out. Read this card before any task whose result leaves this computer (an outside request, a proposal, a talk, an export, a review); it does not say what is true about your research, only what may be used where.

## Material never given to an assistant (by kind and where it lives)

- One kind per line, with where it is: e.g. "manuscripts I review (~/Reviews/): never opened or pasted, not even in part". Never the content itself.

## Not said outside (topic only)

- One topic per line and until when: e.g. "results not yet submitted", "a collaborator's unannounced plans, until they announce them". Name the topic, never the detail.

## Rules on AI tools (with where each is written)

- Who set it (institution, ethics board, funder, journal), what it says in one line, and where it is written (document, section, date checked).

## Who approves before something goes out

- Which kind of output needs whose agreement: e.g. "anything that mentions a shared dataset: its owner".

## Assistant's additions (not confirmed by me)

- Questions an assistant raised about these boundaries; not rules until you answer them and move them up.

## To verify

- Rules you have not looked up yet, or that may have changed.

## Evidence boundary

- This card is based on: what you said, and the rule documents named above.

- Check before an outward task when in doubt (not read by default): the rule documents themselves.
