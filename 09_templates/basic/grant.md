# Short name of the application

## Agent Summary

One or two sentences that stand on their own: which call, what the application proposes and where it stands; when to read this card and what not to use it for.

## The call (fact)

- Scope, eligibility, page and budget limits, review criteria, each with the section of the call text it comes from.

## Fit (my view)

- Why this call fits your work, and where it fits less well.

## Aims

- One line per aim.

## Feasibility

- What already exists (results, data, people) and what still has to be built, with links to project cards.

## Budget notes

- The main cost items and the limits that apply; final figures stay in the budget file.

## Status and next step

- Where the application stands, and the next step with a date.

## Assistant's additions (not confirmed by me)

- An assistant's suggestions go here and are not relied on until confirmed.

## To verify

- Rules in the call you have not checked, figures not yet confirmed.
