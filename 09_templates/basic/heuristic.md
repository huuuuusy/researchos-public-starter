# One sentence stating the habit

## Agent Summary

One or two sentences: what the habit is and when it applies; it is a rule of thumb, not a requirement.

## Rule

- One or two sentences saying what to do.

## Why

- What problem it avoids.

## When to use

- Where it applies.

## When not to use

- Where it does not apply or would mislead.

## Examples

- Links to decision or meeting cards where it was used.

## Risks

- Problems the habit can cause.

## Evidence boundary

- How many examples support it so far, and why `confidence` is set as it is.
