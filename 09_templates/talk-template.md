# Talk title

## Agent Summary

One or two sentences that stand on their own: what the talk was, for whom, and its main message; when to read this card and what not to use it for. Talk phrasing is not paper evidence.

## Basic information (fact)

- Date, event, audience, length, where the slides are.

## What the talk said (fact)

- The core message and the main claims, each with the slide it is on.

## My view

- What worked for this audience, what to change next time.

## Claims that need paper evidence

- Claims made in the talk that a paper or project card has to support before they are reused in writing.

## To verify

- Dates, numbers or claims not yet checked against the slides or the papers.
