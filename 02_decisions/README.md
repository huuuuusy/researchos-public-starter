# Decision Layer

Records public-safe decision examples and confirmation records. Do not add real review, grant, student, collaboration, or internal strategy decisions.

In your own vault, `personal_vault/02_decisions/` holds one card per decision worth keeping: the options, why this one, and the risks accepted. Skeleton: [`09_templates/basic/decision.md`](../09_templates/basic/decision.md); example: [decision card](../examples/starter-cards/en/02_decisions/2026-09-22-wait-for-benchmark-v2.md).
