# ResearchOS Public Starter V1.0.3

Status: released as `v1.0.3` on 2026-10-30. It is a documentation release: the software behaviour of `v1.0.2` is unchanged.

## What Changed

Technical report V11 was corrected after three independent checks: a full run of the worked session against the report, a paragraph-level comparison of the English and Chinese texts, and a check of the citations against their sources. The [English PDF](../technical-report.en.pdf) has 31 pages and the [Chinese PDF](../technical-report.zh.pdf) 27.

- The report no longer calls its practice a third one: requirements tracing and living reviews already hand changes back to people, and the report's claim is to do this for a single research judgment.
- The statements on how an item closes and how a basis change during review is treated now match the code.
- The statement on AI-scientist systems in Section 6, Table 4 and Figures 9 and 10 now matches what their papers report.
- Literature statements in Sections 6 and 7 were tightened to their sources.

The comparison and the study of use have not been run. [V1.0 release notes](v1.0.0.md) remain the record of what V1.0 includes and what remains limited.
