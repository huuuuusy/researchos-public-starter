# Knowledge Layer

Stores public-safe material indexes, reading notes, and sample knowledge objects. The public starter uses fictional or sanitized materials by default.

In your own vault, `personal_vault/01_knowledge/` holds paper cards under `papers/`, project cards under `projects/` and direction summaries under `capsules/`, from the skeletons in [`09_templates/basic/`](../09_templates/basic/README.md). Examples: [paper](../examples/starter-cards/en/01_knowledge/papers/example-benchmark.md), [project](../examples/starter-cards/en/01_knowledge/projects/occlusion-tracking.md), [direction summary](../examples/starter-cards/en/01_knowledge/capsules/occlusion-tracking-capsule.md).
