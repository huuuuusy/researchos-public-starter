# Code of Conduct

ResearchOS Public Starter is a small independent research-tooling project. The community around it should remain careful, constructive, and public-safe.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful when discussing research workflows, tool choices, or design tradeoffs.
- Assume good faith, but keep claims specific and evidence-based.
- Use public-safe examples instead of private research data, unpublished work, personal information, provider keys, or sensitive logs.
- Make feedback actionable: describe what was unclear, what failed, and what would have helped.
- Respect the license boundary for academic, personal, non-commercial, and commercial use.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing another person's private information or sensitive research material.
- Posting credentials, provider tokens, exploit details, or unsafe reproduction data in public issues.
- Misrepresenting the project as an autonomous research system, a production service, or an OSI-approved open source project.
- Using the repository to solicit commercial deployment, hosted services, or large-scale redistribution without prior permission.

## Reporting

For ordinary conduct concerns, contact the maintainer through the trusted channel where you received the project or release information.

For security-sensitive concerns, do not open a public issue. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove unsafe content, close issues, decline pull requests, or restrict participation when needed to protect the public release boundary and keep the project useful for research users.

This code of conduct is inspired by common open-source community standards and adapted for a source-available academic research starter.
