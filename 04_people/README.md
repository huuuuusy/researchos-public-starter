# People Layer

The public starter allows only fictional people or public-safe personas for context-governance demos. Do not add real sensitive people or relationship notes.

In your own vault, `personal_vault/04_people/collaborators/` holds one card per collaborator, with only what papers show or you state, and `sensitivity: sensitive`. Skeleton: [`09_templates/basic/person.md`](../09_templates/basic/person.md); example: [person card](../examples/starter-cards/en/04_people/collaborators/lin-ke.md).
