# Research Work Workflows

These are short methods for everyday research work: reading, writing, reviewing, proposing, following up meetings, advising, deciding on a change of topic, preparing talks and figures, and looking back on a piece of work. Each one says which of your cards an assistant reads at the quick, standard and evidence levels, the steps it follows, what you get, and which parts wait for your confirmation. The route id is what `python3 scripts/plan_context.py <route id> --vault personal_vault` takes.

| Workflow | When to use | Route id |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Paper Reading](paper-reading.md) | Turning one paper, report or chapter into a paper card | `material_understanding` |
| [Paper Writing](paper-writing.md) | Drafting or revising a section of your own paper | `paper_writing` |
| [Peer Review](peer-review.md) | Reviewing a manuscript, chapter or application for someone else | `peer_review` |
| [Grant Proposal](grant-proposal.md) | Planning or drafting a funding application | `proposal` |
| [Meeting Follow-up](meeting-follow-up.md) | Turning meeting notes into decisions, actions and card updates | `meeting_follow_up` |
| [Mentee Feedback](mentee-feedback.md) | Responding to a student's draft, plan or progress update | `mentee_feedback` |
| [Topic Change](topic-change.md) | Deciding whether to continue, narrow, redirect or stop a project | `idea_diagnosis` |
| [Talks and Figures](talks-and-figures.md) | Preparing a talk, deck, poster or paper figure | `presentation`, `figure_design` |
| [Retrospective](retrospective.md) | Looking back on a submission, a declined proposal, a failed experiment or a stalled revision | `system_reflection` |
