# Group Composition Lifecycle Standard

## Agent Summary

This standard explains how ResearchOS turns isolated cards into groups, relations, projects, and directions. A group is not a collection folder. It must answer a concrete question and preserve evidence boundaries for every card it uses.

## Levels

| Level | Name | Typical object |
| --- | --- | --- |
| G0 | Atomic card | paper, person, talk, raw material card |
| G1 | Base group | a small relation or project-family group |
| G2 | Direction group | a higher-level theme, method, or thesis |
| R | Research mainline | the user's top-level research identity or operating thesis |

## Group Requirements

- purpose;
- cards included;
- role of each card;
- evidence-backed links;
- candidate links;
- missing evidence;
- next action;
- validation check;
- export boundary.

## Rule

Do not create a group only because cards look similar. Create a group when the cards jointly answer a question.

## Dissolution Rule

If a group is removed, first migrate useful evidence, decisions, and lessons back to the relevant cards. Do not delete the source group without review.
