# Identity Layer

Holds public-safe project positioning, principles, and use boundaries. Do not place real personal identity, institutional relationships, or non-public biography here.

In your own vault, `personal_vault/00_identity/` holds your profile card (`profile.md`, from [`09_templates/basic/person.md`](../09_templates/basic/person.md) with `relationship_type: self`) and your research identity page (`research-identity.md`, from [`09_templates/basic/research-identity.md`](../09_templates/basic/research-identity.md)), the entry an assistant reads first. Example: [research identity](../examples/starter-cards/en/00_identity/research-identity.md).
